# Human-agent contract

## The person contributes

- physical observations unavailable to the browser;
- corrections to model, fit, and environmental assumptions;
- acceptable budget and risk;
- the final approval decision;
- post-repair outcome evidence.

## The agent contributes

- structured case reading;
- bounded catalog search;
- model compatibility checks;
- evidence-weighted ranking;
- concise comparison;
- staged, reversible planning;
- explicit requests for human judgment;
- verification support after approval.

## Neither participant should do silently

- convert uncertain evidence into certainty;
- hide incompatible options inside a semantic match;
- erase assumptions;
- treat a staged proposal as approved;
- perform high-risk electrical work through the demo.

The product becomes meaningfully better only when these complementary roles operate on the same visible state.
